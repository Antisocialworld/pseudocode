# What lateFee is supposed to do (written by the A3 partner, an AI)

A customer owes a balance in naira and is some number of days late.

- The first 3 days late are a grace period: no fee.
- After the grace period, the fee is 2 percent of the balance for each FULL week the payment is late. A part of a week does not count.
- The fee is never more than 10 percent of the balance.
- The fee is rounded to the nearest 0.01 naira.
